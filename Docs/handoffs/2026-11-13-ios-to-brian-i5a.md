## Contract issues found
- One new row in Docs/contract-issues.md (row 3, open, not blocking): the spec documents no 422 for createBudget, updateBudget and duplicateBudget, although Docs/error-codes.md lists 6003 RECIPIENT_BUDGET_SUM_EXCEEDS_TOTAL as a 422 from the budget service. The generated client therefore delivers a 422 on those three operations as `.undocumented(statusCode: 422, _)`. iOS maps it by status to the typed "limits add up to more than the total" error and shows the fixed fallback text; it does not hand-parse the body, so the server's own message is not shown for that case. Proposal for the API team: document 422 on the three operations. deleteBudget documents its 422 (6004) and is typed.
- API facts relied on (all agreed between the spec, the guide and error-codes.md except row 3): stats and budgets are the caller's own data; GET /stats?year= returns the year scope with `budget` null when the year has no budget; createBudget needs year, totalBudget and a recipients array that may be empty; updateBudget with recipients replaces the whole allocation; deleteBudget is refused with 6004 while purchases exist in that year; duplicateBudget takes toYear; 409 BUDGET_ALREADY_EXISTS (5003) for create and duplicate; money is a number with at most two decimals. No behavior was observed against a real server in this package (no network access in the session).

### Known wording difference in step (1) (the check text above is unchanged)
Step (1) expects a fresh John with nothing spent to see a "No budget for <year>" card. The built screen follows the brief's Decisions: with no purchases and no budget in the year it shows the short explanation "Nothing spent in <year> yet. Create a budget to plan your spending." with the Create Budget button and no summary card; the "No budget for <year>" card appears once the year has purchases but no budget. So at step (1) expect the explanation card (the Create Budget button and "nothing spent" parts match), and the "No budget for <year>" card after a purchase in a year without a budget. Steps (8) and (11) show the same explanation card. This is a disagreement inside the brief (Decisions versus the check text), not a code defect; decide whether to reword the check or change the empty state.
