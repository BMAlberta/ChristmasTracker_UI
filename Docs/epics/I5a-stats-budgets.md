# I5a: The Statistics tab and budgets
Track: iOS. Depends on: I4c (merged). Blocks: nothing (the purchase history screen is a later package).
Contract state: CONFORMANT at decisions revision 34. API models come only from the generated client. No contract change in this package.

Why: the Statistics tab is a placeholder ("Statistics coming soon"), and no screen can create a budget, so the budget warnings recorded in I4a can never fire for a real user. This package builds the Statistics tab (what you spent this year, by person, against your budget) and the budget management: create, edit, duplicate to another year, and delete.

Server facts (client guide 5.4, 7.8, 7.9, the privacy matrix, error codes; confirm each against the synced files and the spec): stats and budgets are strictly the caller's OWN data (GET /stats, GET /budgets, GET /budgets/{year} and the writes are own-only; another user's budget is a 404). GET /stats?year= returns the year stats: scope, year, totalSpent, itemsPurchased, purchaseCount, spendByRecipient [{recipientId, displayName, spent, itemsPurchased}], budget (null when the year has no budget; otherwise totalBudget, totalSpent, remaining and per-recipient rows) and availableYears. A budget is one per user per year: createBudget takes year (2000 to 2100), totalBudget (a money amount, 0 to 999999.99, at most two decimals) and recipients [{recipientId, maxSpend}] (the array is required but MAY BE EMPTY, so a total-only budget is valid; the sum of the maxSpend values must not exceed totalBudget); it returns 201 with the budget. updateBudget (PATCH /budgets/{year}) takes optional totalBudget and optional recipients; when recipients is sent it REPLACES the whole allocation; the sum rule is checked against the new or the existing total. getBudget and listBudgets return each budget with real-time spending: id, year, totalBudget, totalSpent, remaining, isOverBudget, recipients [{id, recipientId, recipientName, maxSpend, spent, remaining, isOverBudget, overage}], createdAt, updatedAt. deleteBudget (DELETE /budgets/{year}) is refused while the caller has any purchase in that year. duplicateBudget (POST /budgets/{year}/duplicate with toYear) copies the total and the recipient limits (not the purchases) to a new year. Errors: 400 validation (including 3007 INVALID_PRICE for a bad amount), 401, 403 ACCOUNT_DISABLED (ends the session through the existing seam), 404 BUDGET_NOT_FOUND (4007, treat as not found), 409 BUDGET_ALREADY_EXISTS (5003, show the message; also for a duplicate to a year that has a budget), 422 RECIPIENT_BUDGET_SUM_EXCEEDS_TOTAL (6003, show the message), 422 CANNOT_DELETE_BUDGET_WITH_PURCHASES (6004, show the message), 429, 500. Spend is counted per purchaser and per list year; a per-recipient limit only applies to purchases whose recipient is that person. The seeded development data has budgets for Jane (total 1000: John 200, Tommy 700, Mike 100) and Mike (total 600: Tommy 600); Mike is already over his Tommy limit (619.98 against 600). The API does NOT say whom a user may set a limit for: the recipients are the people the user buys gifts for, so the app derives the choices itself (see Decisions).

Scope
1. Story A: domain types and pure rules (budget draft validation with Decimal, recipient options, stats and budget view states).
2. Story B: generated-client repositories, mocks and session-safe services for stats and budgets, and the cache invalidation hook after a purchase changes.
3. Story C: the Statistics tab, the budget editor, duplicate, and delete.
4. Story D: handoff and the real-mode check.

Decisions
- UI RULES FOR NEW CODE (banked polish backlog; they apply to everything new or rewritten in this package; older screens are not refactored): (1) every confirmationDialog is attached to the control that triggers it (for the delete confirmation: the Delete control or the menu button that opens the delete action), never to a List, ScrollView, Form, NavigationStack, or screen container, and its presentation state lives in a stable owner above any row; (2) errors are scoped: a mutation error (create, edit, duplicate, delete a budget) is thrown by the service to the initiating view and held in its local state (an InlineErrorBanner inside the sheet or section that started it) and disappears on dismiss, retry success, closing the sheet, or leaving the screen; a load error is its own service property for the Statistics screen only, cleared on a new load and on disappear; nothing is published in a shared error property that another screen reads; (3) destructive actions use role: .destructive with Color.destructiveColor; (4) errors use InlineErrorBanner; a stale-data failure (a refresh that fails while saved data is shown) shows "Couldn't update. Showing saved data." with Try Again and Dismiss, and a load with nothing to show shows the full error state with Try Again.
- Names: BudgetService and StatsService are the session-safe @MainActor @Observable services (if a name is taken, pick a clear alternative and say so). Domain types: Budget, RecipientBudget, StatsYear, RecipientSpend, BudgetDraft, BudgetRecipientDraft, BudgetValidation, BudgetRecipientOption. Money is Decimal end to end (the package helpers convert at the repository boundary; never arithmetic on a raw Double).
- The Statistics tab replaces the placeholder. Layout: a year picker (a menu of the years from availableYears, the years of the budgets, and the current year, newest first; the default is the current year even when it is not in availableYears); a summary card (total spent, items purchased, purchases); a budget card; and a Spend by person list. Pull to refresh forces the fetch; the first open is cache-first. Empty states: no purchases and no budget in the year shows a short explanation and the Create Budget button; purchases without a budget shows the summary and the "No budget for <year>" card with Create Budget.
- The budget card with a budget shows total, spent, remaining (or, when over, "Over by <amount>" in text, never by color alone, with the over-budget token styling as well), a progress bar (spent over total, capped visually at full when over), and one row per person with the limit, what was spent, and what remains or the overage. A menu on the card offers Edit Budget, Duplicate to Another Year, and Delete Budget (destructive, red).
- Recipient options (people you can set a limit for): a pure function builds them from (a) the distinct recipients of the user's lists where canPurchase is true (the people the user can buy for; the user's own blind lists never appear), (b) the recipients already in the budget being edited, and (c) the recipients in the year's spendByRecipient; de-duplicated by id, with display names from the first source that has one, sorted by name. The editor offers only options not yet used in the budget. The lists are loaded cache-first through the ListService when the editor opens; if they are loading or failed, the picker shows that state and the user can still save a total-only budget (the recipients array may be empty).
- Budget editor (create and edit, one sheet): create: the year (a picker of years that do NOT already have a budget, from the previous year to three years ahead, default the current year if it has none, otherwise the next free year), the total budget, and the per-person limits; edit: the year is read-only and shown as the title. Fields: total (a Decimal text field with the form's locale-aware parser from the purchase form's rules: at most two decimals, 0 to 999999.99), and a row per chosen person (name, a limit field, a remove button) with an "Add person" menu of unused options. A live summary line: "Limits <sum> of <total>; <unallocated> unallocated", turning into an error text "Limits add up to more than the total" (and Save disabled) when the sum exceeds the total. Rules checked client-side with Decimal: the total and every limit are valid amounts; no person twice; the sum of the limits does not exceed the total; Save is enabled only when valid and (for edit) something changed. Create sends year, totalBudget and recipients (possibly empty). Edit sends ONLY what changed: totalBudget when it changed, and recipients (the full list) when any limit or the set of people changed. After a successful create or edit the Statistics screen shows the returned budget immediately and refreshes the stats for that year. 409 shows the message inside the sheet (for create); 422 sum errors show the message inside the sheet.
- Duplicate: a small sheet offering the years that do not have a budget (from the next year to three years ahead; default the next free year) with a Duplicate button; success selects the new year on the Statistics screen; 409 shows the message inside the sheet.
- Delete: a confirmation dialog attached to the control that opens it with the title "Delete the <year> budget?", the message "Your spending is not affected. This can't be undone.", and the destructive button "Delete Budget"; success removes the budget from the screen and the caches and refreshes the stats; 422 (purchases exist) shows the server's message as an InlineErrorBanner on the budget card and the budget stays; a 404 counts as already deleted.
- Cache and invalidation: StatsService caches the year stats per year and BudgetService caches the budgets, both cleared at teardown and registered with the session clearables (update the pinned clearable count and its test with a stated reason). Recording or deleting a purchase changes spending: add ONE step to PurchaseRefreshPlan (and run it through the existing refresh runner) that marks the stats and budget caches stale, so the Statistics screen refetches the next time it appears; this is the only change to purchase code and it needs a test.
- Session safety: generation, cancellation, nothing written after the session ended or a user switch, stale results discarded, mutation methods throw typed errors and publish nothing. 401 is handled by the middleware; 403 ACCOUNT_DISABLED and 404 USER_NOT_FOUND by the existing session-ending seam.
- No amounts, names, or ids in any log, Sentry event, or accessibility identifier. VoiceOver: the progress bar has a label and value ("Spent <amount> of <total>"); the over-budget text is read; each person row is one element.
- Mock mode: Mock repositories behave like the server (own data only, one budget per year, the sum rule, delete refused when mock purchases exist, duplicate copies the structure, 409 and 422 as above) with deterministic data; the mock does not move item or purchase data when budgets change (documented limitation).

Non-goals: the purchase history screen and its filters and paging (a later package), charts beyond the progress bar, budgets for other users, push or email alerts, editing the year of a budget, recurring budgets, changing the purchase budget warning behavior, refactoring older screens, the polish and theme passes.

Acceptance
- Through the REAL factory-built client with a fake transport: the year stats, listBudgets, getBudget, createBudget, updateBudget, deleteBudget and duplicateBudget map every documented response (success, 400 including INVALID_PRICE, 401, 403, 404 BUDGET_NOT_FOUND, 409 BUDGET_ALREADY_EXISTS, 422 for both codes, 429, 500, undocumented, transport failure) with guide-accurate fixtures including the full meta object, a stats response with and without a budget, and a budget with and without recipients; requests carry exactly the fields the builder says (create: year, totalBudget, recipients possibly empty; edit: only the changed fields; duplicate: toYear); money crosses the boundary as Decimal.
- The pure rules have full truth tables: the budget draft validation (valid; total zero, negative, 0.01, 999999.99, 1000000.00, three decimals; a limit over the total; the sum equal to the total and one cent over; a person twice; empty recipients), the edit diff (nothing changed, only the total, only a limit, a person added, a person removed), the recipient options (from lists with canPurchase true and false, blind own lists excluded, existing allocations kept, spend recipients merged, de-duplication, name selection, sorting), the year choices (with and without existing budgets, the current-year default, the free-year default), and the stats view state (no purchases and no budget; purchases without a budget; a budget under, at, and over the total; a person over their limit; the zero-total budget).
- The over-budget presentation never relies on color alone (text "Over by ..." present in every over case).
- Create, edit, duplicate and delete update the screen from the returned data and refresh the stats; a failed mutation shows its banner inside its sheet or card and never on another screen; deleting a budget with purchases keeps it and shows the message.
- A recorded or deleted purchase marks the stats and budget caches stale (a test on PurchaseRefreshPlan and the runner).
- Stale data from a previous session or user is impossible (teardown, user switch, cancellation); nothing is written after the session ended.
- The confirmation dialog is attached to its control; destructive controls use the token; no ad-hoc error row; no amount, name, or id is logged or placed in an accessibility identifier (scan tests).
- No change under contract/, .claude/, any .pbxproj, the synced Docs files, or LocalPackages/.
